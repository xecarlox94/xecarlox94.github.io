Between the two, the **second one is generally stronger**:

> **Rust-focused Systems Software Engineer with 4 years of hands-on experience delivering efficient, secure, and maintainable software solutions across the systems stack.**

Why it works better:

* It leads with your specialization (**Rust-focused**), which immediately tells recruiters what you're known for.
* "Hands-on experience delivering" emphasizes practical impact rather than just expertise.
* The qualities "efficient, secure, and maintainable" are highly valued in systems engineering.
* "Across the systems stack" suggests breadth without sounding overly broad.

The first version is also good:

> Experienced Systems Software Engineer bringing 4 years of professional expertise in Rust development, distributed systems, and performance-critical software.

This version is stronger if your target roles heavily emphasize **distributed systems** and **performance engineering**.

You can combine the best parts of both:

> **Rust-focused Systems Software Engineer with 4 years of experience building efficient, secure, and maintainable distributed systems and performance-critical software.**

This version is concise, technical, and recruiter-friendly.
